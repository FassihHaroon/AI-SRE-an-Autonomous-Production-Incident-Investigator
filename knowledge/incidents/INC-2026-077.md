# INC-2026-077: Failed canary of api-gateway left broken replicas serving 25% of traffic

> Synthetic postmortem for the simulated environment (training/evaluation data).

## What happened

A config parsing change rejected an existing value; canary replicas failed every request. The rollout was marked failed but canaries were not scaled down.

## How it was detected and diagnosed

Uniform 500s across all routes only from the new version (log version field).

## Resolution

Rollout aborted, canaries removed. Pipeline now scales canaries to zero on failure.
