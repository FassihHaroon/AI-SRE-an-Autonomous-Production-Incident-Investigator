# INC-2026-058: Payments provider degradation caused order placement failures

> Synthetic postmortem for the simulated environment (training/evaluation data).

## What happened

The third-party PSP returned 503 for roughly a third of charges with elevated latency. Orders failed with 502; no internal change.

## How it was detected and diagnosed

Errors only on POST /orders; outbound http_client errors to payments-provider; provider status page confirmed after 20 minutes. Rolling back would not have helped.

## Resolution

Waited out the provider incident; added alerting on upstream error ratio.
